Quiet Feed settings

What gets sent to TypeSafe

When filtering is on, Quiet Feed sends each post it evaluates on your X home timeline to TypeSafe (api.typesafe.ai) using your API key: the post text, any quoted-post text, image descriptions and labels, whether a video is attached, and the wording of the filters and topics you enabled.

It does not send direct messages, drafts, cookies, your account details, or anything else on the page. Decisions are cached on this device for 24 hours as hashes and scores; post text is not stored.

AI video filtering is based on text and labels only. Quiet Feed cannot see video footage.

API key

"Remember" stores the key in this extension's local storage, readable only by the extension's own pages and service worker. It is not encrypted and is never synced or exported.

Custom topics

Each topic is judged separately. Exceptions keep matching posts visible, for example "Hide cryptocurrency price speculation; keep technical blockchain research."
